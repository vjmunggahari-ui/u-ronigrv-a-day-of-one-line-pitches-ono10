Entrants write one sentence, 240 characters at most, that would convince a friend who has never coded to let an AI agent build their first app idea on make.fun. It is the whole entry: no code, no explanation. A house judge compares sentences two at a time on persuasiveness, clarity and truth in one read, in private blind rounds spread across the day. Entries stay open 24 hours, which draws a larger field of pitches, and the top three take the $5 reward, $2, $2 and $1. Free to enter.

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
